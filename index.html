Ghost Image

Follow the links on the left to view some photographic experiments as part of an ongoing work in progress.

Follow the link on the right to upload a photograph of your own. The program will pair it at random with a photograph from the base library and layer the two together. You might get one of mine or one that another visitor has left. You can also choose to leave your own photograph in the library for someone else to work with.
